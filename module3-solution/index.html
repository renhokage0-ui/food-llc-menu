01 / From the kitchen

Chicken

Golden, comforting, and always a crowd favorite.

There is a reason chicken has a place at almost every family table. It can be crisp and golden, gently roasted, or brightened with a squeeze of lemon. Our kitchen starts with that simple idea: good ingredients, thoughtful seasoning, and enough time to let every flavor shine.

Lemon & Herb Roast $18

Slow-roasted chicken with lemon, rosemary, and a golden finish. Served with warm potatoes and seasonal vegetables.

Crispy Chicken Plate $17

A crunchy, seasoned coating with a tender center. Finished with house slaw, pickles, and a little honey mustard.

Smoky Grilled Chicken $19

Grilled over a hot flame, brushed with a smoky glaze, and served with roasted corn and a fresh green salad.

Chicken & Rice Bowl $15

Warm rice, grilled chicken, crisp cucumber, and herbs. A tangy yogurt dressing brings everything together.

Garlic Butter Chicken $20

Pan-seared chicken in a garlic butter sauce, with creamy mashed potatoes and a generous handful of fresh parsley.

Garden Chicken Salad $14

Leafy greens, cherry tomatoes, toasted seeds, and sliced chicken, tossed with our lemon vinaigrette.

A little more about our chicken

Our favorite plates balance rich, savory flavors with something fresh. A roast comes with seasonal vegetables; a crispy plate gets a lively slaw; and a rice bowl brings plenty of crunchy greens. Each pairing is designed to make the last bite as enjoyable as the first.

The lemon and herb roast is the kind of meal that invites you to slow down. We let the herbs perfume the chicken as it cooks, then bring it to the table with its warm accompaniments. It is a good choice for a relaxed lunch or a dinner shared with friends.

For a lighter meal, try the garden salad or our chicken and rice bowl. Both keep the comforting flavor of chicken at the center while adding texture from fresh vegetables. The dressing arrives on the side, so you can finish the plate just the way you like it.

Looking for a little extra warmth? Ask for our house pepper sauce. Prefer something mild? The garlic butter plate and herb roast are gentle favorites. Our team can help you choose a side or explain how a dish is prepared before you order.

Good food is even better in good company.

02 / Hearty favorites

Beef

Rich flavors, simple preparations, generous plates.

From a classic burger to a slowly simmered stew, our beef menu celebrates meals that feel familiar and satisfying. We keep the preparations straightforward, build flavor patiently, and pair every plate with sides that make sense.

The House Burger $17

A grilled beef patty, cheddar, crisp lettuce, tomato, and house sauce on a toasted bun, with golden fries.

Steak & Potatoes $28

Seared steak with roasted potatoes, seasonal greens, and a spoonful of our herb butter.

Slow-Cooked Beef Stew $21

Beef gently simmered with carrots, onions, and potatoes in a rich broth. Served with crusty bread.

Beef & Pepper Bowl $18

Sliced beef, sweet peppers, and onions over warm rice, finished with a savory sauce and fresh herbs.

Roast Beef Sandwich $16

Thinly sliced roast beef, peppery greens, caramelized onion, and horseradish cream on toasted bread.

Beef Meatballs $19

Tender meatballs in a slow-simmered tomato sauce, served over pasta with grated cheese and basil.

Comfort, one plate at a time

Some meals are best when you give them time. Our stew begins with a gentle simmer, allowing the broth, vegetables, and beef to come together gradually. The result is a warming bowl with plenty of depth, perfect alongside a piece of bread.

The house burger is our answer to an everyday classic. A toasted bun keeps its texture, fresh vegetables add crunch, and a modest layer of sauce ties it all together. You can add an extra portion of pickles or exchange the fries for a green salad.

If you prefer a plate centered on the beef itself, choose steak and potatoes. Tell our team how you like your steak prepared when you order. The potatoes bring a crisp edge, while the greens and herb butter make a balanced accompaniment.

Our bowls and sandwiches offer the same hearty flavors in a more casual format. They make a comfortable lunch, whether you are taking a break on your own or catching up with a friend. Ask us about the vegetables and sauces available that day.

Classic dishes, made with a little extra care.

03 / Fresh & balanced

Sushi

Delicate rolls, bright flavors, beautifully simple bites.

Our sushi menu brings together carefully seasoned rice, crisp vegetables, and delicious fillings. Whether you choose a familiar roll or a mixed plate to share, each bite is designed to feel balanced, fresh, and easy to enjoy.

Salmon Avocado Roll $13

Salmon and creamy avocado wrapped in seasoned rice and nori, with a light sprinkle of sesame.

Cucumber Garden Roll $10

A crisp combination of cucumber, carrot, and avocado. A bright, vegetable-forward favorite.

Spicy Tuna Roll $14

Tuna, cucumber, and a touch of spicy sauce, rolled with rice and finished with sliced spring onion.

Shrimp Tempura Roll $15

Crispy shrimp, avocado, and cucumber, with a light savory glaze and a satisfying contrast of textures.

Salmon Nigiri $12

Four pieces of salmon over small, carefully shaped portions of seasoned sushi rice.

Kitchen Sharing Plate $32

A selection of our favorite rolls and nigiri, arranged for two people to discover and share.

Small bites, plenty of choice

A good roll is about the way its ingredients work together. Crisp cucumber offers contrast to soft rice; avocado adds creaminess; and a small amount of sauce brings the flavors into focus. We keep the balance in mind as we assemble every order.

If sushi is new to you, the cucumber garden roll is an approachable place to start. The shrimp tempura roll adds a crisp, cooked filling, while salmon avocado is a familiar favorite. Our team is happy to describe the different options.

For an unhurried meal with a friend, try the kitchen sharing plate. It includes a variety of textures and flavors, making it easy to find a new favorite together. Soy sauce, ginger, and wasabi are served separately so you can decide how much to add.

Vegetable rolls are available alongside our seafood selections. Tell our team about any ingredient preferences or allergies before ordering, and ask us to explain preparation and cross-contact considerations so you can make an informed choice.

Find your favorite. Then share another.
